FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work? +
You send footage → I edit → you review → final delivery. Simple and clear.
Do you offer revisions? +
Yes! I offer 2–3 revisions depending on the project. If extra adjustments are needed, I’ll continue refining the edit until you’re 100% happy with the final result.
What videos do you edit? +
I currently edit content for AI automation creators, fitness creators, and writers. Some videos are high-energy edits, while others are clean and simple raw-cut styles depending on the creator’s brand.
Do you edit short-form content? +
Yes! I specialize in short-form content like TikToks, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. If you want high-retention short-form edits, I offer dedicated packages for that.
What’s the cost? +
Pricing depends on the type of content, editing complexity, and turnaround time. Contact me with your project details and I’ll give you a clear quote upfront.
How fast is delivery? +
Most edits are delivered within 48 hours depending on the project size and complexity. Rush delivery options are also available.
Why choose me? ×
Because I deliver creative edits, fast communication, and results that grab attention.
